## 1. Regulatory Framework
This document outlines the classification rationale for the Naomi NHS Parenting Chatbot under the UK Medical Devices Regulations 2002 (UK MDR 2002) (SI 2002 No 618, as amended). It applies the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) guidance on medical device stand-alone software (Software as a Medical Device, SaMD), which reflects the International Medical Device Regulators Forum (IMDRF) framework.

## 2. Intended Purpose Statement
The Naomi NHS Parenting Chatbot is an Artificial Intelligence-powered software application designed to provide NHS-grounded guidance and informational support to parents and carers of children aged 0–5 within the United Kingdom. Naomi processes user queries to deliver accurate, evidence-based responses drawn from trusted NHS sources, assisting parents in making informed decisions about their children's well-being and development. Naomi is intended for use by laypersons (parents and carers). It is not intended to provide definitive clinical diagnoses, prescribe treatment, or replace professional medical consultation.

## 3. MHRA/IMDRF SaMD Classification Application
Under the MHRA guidance on medical device software classification (referencing IMDRF N12 framework), Naomi’s functionality involves the processing of data to provide information that assists in health management. 

- **Significance of Information Provided:** The information provided is informational and supportive, aimed at assisting healthcare management rather than driving immediate critical clinical interventions.
- **State of Healthcare Situation:** The target demographic involves children aged 0-5 in primarily non-critical, everyday parenting scenarios.

Therefore, the software falls into a lower risk category within the SaMD framework, but specific rules under the UK MDR 2002 must be evaluated.

## 4. Classification Determination (UK MDR 2002)
Naomi functions as an active medical device. Evaluating the device against the classification rules detailed in Part II, Schedule 2 of the UK MDR 2002:

- **Rule 9:** Active therapeutic devices intended to administer or exchange energy are Class IIa/IIb. (Not applicable).
- **Rule 10:** Active devices intended for diagnosis are in Class IIa if they are intended to supply information which will be used to take decisions with diagnosis or therapeutic purposes. Naomi supplies information that parents use to manage their child's health and development. While Naomi acts as an informational chatbot, it borders on providing data used for health decisions. Given the guidance on AI and diagnostic/therapeutic informational software, Naomi is likely classified as **Class IIa**. 
- **Rule 11 (MDR reference):** Software intended to provide information which is used to take decisions with diagnosis or therapeutic purposes is classified as Class IIa. Note: Under the current UK MDR 2002 transposed rules (pre-new MDR adoption), software is often classified by analogy. Based on current MHRA guidance and expected future rules mirroring the EU MDR Rule 11, classification as Class IIa is the conservative and appropriate determination.

**Conclusion:** The Naomi NHS Parenting Chatbot is classified as a **Class IIa Medical Device**.

## 5. Conformity Assessment Route
As a Class IIa device, the applicable conformity assessment route under the UK MDR 2002 is **Part II, Annex II (excluding section 4) - Full Quality Assurance System** or **Annex V (Production Quality Assurance) combined with Annex VII**. 
Naomi will proceed under the Annex II (Full Quality Assurance System) equivalent route for UKCA marking, which is comprehensively supported by our ISO 13485:2016 compliant Quality Management System.

## 6. Essential Requirements
Naomi complies with the Essential Requirements laid out in Schedule 1 of the UK MDR 2002. Specifically:
- **General Requirements (1-6):** Ensuring the device does not compromise clinical condition or safety, and risks are minimised.
- **Requirements regarding design and construction (Part II, sections 7.1, 9, 12.1a):** Naomi’s software lifecycle is managed via IEC 62304. Validation and verification are conducted to ensure repeatability, reliability, and performance. Risk management follows ISO 14971.

## 7. Notified Body and MHRA Registration Requirement
- **Approved Body Requirement:** As a Class IIa device, intervention by a UK Approved Body is mandatory to perform the conformity assessment and issue a UKCA certificate.
- **MHRA Registration:** Prior to being placed on the Great Britain market, the Naomi chatbot must be registered with the MHRA. The organisation must obtain a Data Universal Numbering System (DUNS) number and register the device via the MHRA Device Registration System, ensuring ongoing compliance and post-market surveillance reporting.
