## 1. Purpose and Scope
In accordance with ISO 13485:2016 Clause 8.2.4, this procedure establishes the process for planning, conducting, reporting, and following up on internal audits. The purpose is to verify that the Naomi Quality Management System (QMS) conforms to ISO 13485, IEC 62304, regulatory requirements (UK MDR, NHS DTAC), and the organisation's own documented procedures, and is effectively implemented and maintained.

## 2. Audit Programme
An Internal Audit Programme is established annually by the Quality Management Representative (QMR). 
*   **Frequency:** All QMS processes shall be audited at least once annually.
*   **Triggers:** Additional ad-hoc audits may be triggered by significant organisational changes, major software architectural shifts (e.g., migrating from LLaMA 3.1 to a new AI model), negative complaint trends, or post-market safety incidents.

## 3. Audit Criteria
Audits evaluate objective evidence against the following criteria:
*   ISO 13485:2016 standard requirements.
*   IEC 62304:2015 Software lifecycle processes.
*   Applicable UK MDR 2002 requirements.
*   Internal QMS documentation (policies, procedures, SOPs).

## 4. Auditor Independence
Auditors must be objective and impartial. **An auditor shall not audit their own work.** Given the small size of the organisation (<20 employees), external consultants or cross-departmental peer reviews (e.g., a frontend developer auditing QMS record control, guided by the QMR) may be utilised to maintain this mandated independence.

## 5. Audit Planning and Preparation
The Lead Auditor develops an Audit Plan detailing the scope, criteria, dates, and interviewees. Auditors prepare by reviewing relevant documentation, previous audit findings, and completing the Audit Checklist (QMS-7-04).

## 6. Conduct of Audit
Audits are conducted via document review, observation of processes (e.g., reviewing GitHub pull request approvals, Cloudflare deployment logs), and interviews with relevant personnel. Objective evidence is collected to verify compliance.

## 7. Audit Findings and Report
Upon completion, the auditor categorises findings as:
*   **Major Nonconformity:** A total breakdown of a QMS system or a direct risk to SaMD safety/regulatory compliance.
*   **Minor Nonconformity:** A single lapse in a procedure that does not immediately threaten product safety.
*   **Observation / Opportunity for Improvement (OFI):** A practice that complies with the standard but could be optimised.

An Audit Report is generated summarising the findings and presented to Management.

## 8. Corrective Action Initiation
For all identified nonconformities, the auditee's department head must determine the root cause and propose corrective actions. Major nonconformities, or minor nonconformities indicating a systemic failure, trigger the formal CAPA Procedure (QMS-8.5-01).

## 9. Follow-up Verification
The implementation and effectiveness of corrective actions resulting from internal audits must be verified by the auditor or QMR in a timely manner, typically during the next scheduled audit or within an agreed timeframe for high-severity issues.

## 10. Records
Internal audit schedules, plans, checklists (QMS-7-04), reports, and follow-up verifications shall be maintained as QMS records per ISO 13485 Clause 4.2.5 and QMS-4.2.5-01.

## 11. Terms and Definitions
*   **Audit:** Systematic, independent, and documented process for obtaining objective evidence and evaluating it impartially to determine the extent to which audit criteria are fulfilled.
*   **Audit Criteria:** Set of policies, procedures, or requirements used as a reference against which objective evidence is compared.
*   **Objective Evidence:** Data supporting the existence or verity of something, obtained through observation, measurement, or test.

## 12. Responsibilities
*   **Quality Manager / QMR:** Establishes the annual internal audit schedule, assigns independent auditors, and reports audit outcomes to Management Review.
*   **Lead Auditor:** Prepares audit plans, conducts interviews, reviews objective evidence, and authors audit reports.
*   **Auditee (Department Leads):** Facilitate audit access, provide required records, and remediate identified nonconformities via CAPA (QMS-8.5-01).

## 13. Inputs and Outputs
*   **Inputs:** Annual audit schedule, ISO 13485:2016 standard, previous audit findings, CAPA status reports.
*   **Outputs:** Executed Internal Audit Checklists (QMS-7-04), formal Internal Audit Reports, initiated CAPAs (QMS-7-01).
