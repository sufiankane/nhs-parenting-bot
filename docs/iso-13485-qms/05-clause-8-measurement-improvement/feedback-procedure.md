## 1. Purpose and Scope
In compliance with ISO 13485:2016 Clause 8.2.1, this document outlines the procedure for gathering, monitoring, and analysing feedback regarding the quality, performance, and safety of the Naomi AI chatbot. This procedure ensures early detection of potential issues and drives continuous improvement of the Software as a Medical Device (SaMD).

## 2. Feedback Sources
The organisation proactively and reactively collects feedback from multiple channels, including:
*   **In-App Feedback Form:** A mechanism within Naomi allowing users to flag specific AI responses or provide general comments.
*   **App Store Reviews:** Monitoring ratings and reviews (if distributed via app stores).
*   **NHS Procurement and Clinical Feedback:** Direct input from NHS stakeholders, clinical sponsors, and partner health trusts.
*   **User Satisfaction Surveys:** Periodic, voluntary surveys sent to registered users.
*   **Social Media & Web Monitoring:** Passive monitoring of public mentions regarding the Naomi application.
*   **Customer Support Emails:** Direct communications sent to the organisation's support inbox.

## 3. Collection and Routing Process
All incoming feedback is logged into a central tracking system (e.g., Jira, Zendesk, or dedicated secure CRM) managed by the Quality and Support teams. The Quality representative reviews all new entries within a defined timeframe to ensure prompt categorisation.

## 4. Categorisation of Feedback
Feedback is triaged into one of the following categories:
*   **Feature Request:** Suggestions for new capabilities or integrations.
*   **Usability:** Comments regarding the user interface, ease of use, or accessibility.
*   **Quality/Performance:** Reports of slow response times (e.g., LLaMA 3.1 latency), downtime, or non-safety software bugs.
*   **Safety-Related:** Concerns that the chatbot provided inaccurate, dangerous, or misleading medical advice, or failed to adequately signpost a user in a clinical emergency.

## 5. Escalation and Linkage to QMS
*   **Safety-Related Escalation:** Any feedback categorised as "Safety-Related" or indicative of a software defect that could lead to clinical harm is **immediately** escalated to the Complaint Handling Procedure (QMS-8.2.2-01) for formal investigation and potential regulatory reporting.
*   **General Feedback:** Usability, Feature, and Quality feedback are routed to the Product Management and Engineering teams for evaluation during sprint planning and software lifecycle management.

## 6. Analysis and Trending
Feedback data is aggregated and analysed quarterly by the Quality Management Representative (QMR). The analysis evaluates trends in user satisfaction, recurrence of specific usability issues, and general application performance.

## 7. Linkage to Post-Market Surveillance (PMS)
The aggregated feedback analysis forms a core input into the Post-Market Surveillance Plan (QMS-PMS-01) and the Periodic Safety Update Report (PSUR), ensuring that real-world evidence continuously informs the device's risk management file (ISO 14971).

## 8. Response to Feedback
Where contact information is provided and appropriate, the organisation will acknowledge receipt of the feedback and, if necessary, provide resolution updates to the user.

## 9. Records
Records of feedback, its categorisation, and any subsequent actions or routing shall be maintained in the central tracking system per ISO 13485 Clause 4.2.5 and QMS-4.2.5-01.

## 10. Terms and Definitions
*   **Customer Feedback:** Information relating to customer perception regarding whether the organisation has met customer requirements.
*   **Safety-Related Feedback:** Any comment, report, or observation alleging potential, near-miss, or actual patient harm arising from software interaction.
*   **Trending:** Statistical analysis of feedback frequencies over time to detect shifts in quality or clinical risk.

## 11. Responsibilities
*   **Quality Manager:** Triage feedback, monitors feedback trending, and escalates safety items to complaint handling (QMS-8.2.2-01).
*   **Clinical Safety Officer:** Evaluates clinical feedback and advises on safety implications.
*   **Product Owner:** Incorporates usability and feature feedback into product roadmaps.

## 12. Inputs and Outputs
*   **Inputs:** In-app user ratings, support emails, app store reviews, NHS partner observations.
*   **Outputs:** Feedback triage records, escalated Complaint Records (QMS-7-02), quarterly PMS trend reports.
