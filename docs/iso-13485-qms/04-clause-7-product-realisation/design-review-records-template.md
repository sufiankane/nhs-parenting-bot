## 6. Scope and Applicability
This template applies to all formal design stage-gate reviews conducted throughout the Naomi SaMD development lifecycle.

## 7. Terms and Definitions
*   **Design Review:** Documented, comprehensive, systematic examination of a design to evaluate the adequacy of the design requirements, evaluate the capability of the design to meet these requirements, and identify problems.
*   **Quorum:** Minimum required attendance (Engineering, Quality/Regulatory, and Clinical Safety Officer) necessary to validate the review.

## 8. Responsibilities
*   **Review Chairperson (Quality Manager):** Convenes review, confirms quorum, guides agenda, and documents decisions.
*   **Lead Developer:** Presents engineering deliverables, architectural designs, and verification data.
*   **Clinical Safety Officer:** Evaluates clinical risk control adequacy and safety impacts.

## 9. Inputs and Outputs
*   **Inputs:** Stage gate deliverables (Design Inputs QMS-7.3.3-01, Design Outputs QMS-7.3.4-01, Verification Reports QMS-7.3.6-01, Risk Management File QMS-14971-02).
*   **Outputs:** Formal stage gate approval/rejection decision, documented action items, and completed Design Review Record filed in the DHF (QMS-7.3.10-01).
