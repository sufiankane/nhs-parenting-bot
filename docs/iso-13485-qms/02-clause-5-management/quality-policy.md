# 1. Purpose
This document establishes the Quality Policy for «[INSERT: Organisation Name]» in accordance with ISO 13485:2016 (Clause 5.3). This policy forms the foundation of our Quality Management System (QMS) and reflects our commitment to patient safety, clinical efficacy, and regulatory compliance in the development and operation of the Naomi application.

# 2. Quality Policy Statement
«[INSERT: Organisation Name]» is dedicated to providing Naomi, an AI-powered chatbot that delivers reliable, NHS-grounded guidance for parents and carers of children aged 0–5 in the UK. 

Top Management ensures that this Quality Policy:
a) is appropriate to the purpose of the organisation and the specific nature of our Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) operations;
b) includes a commitment to comply with requirements and to maintain the effectiveness of the QMS;
c) provides a framework for establishing and reviewing quality objectives;
d) is communicated and understood within the organisation; and
e) is reviewed for continuing suitability.

**Our Core Commitments:**
1. **Patient Safety & Clinical Efficacy:** We prioritise the health and safety of end-users and their dependants, ensuring our software outputs are clinically safe, evidence-based, and accurately aligned with NHS guidelines.
2. **Regulatory Compliance:** We are steadfast in our commitment to meet applicable regulatory requirements, including the UK Medical Devices Regulations 2002 (UK MDR), NHS Digital Technology Assessment Criteria (DTAC), and clinical risk management standards such as DCB0129.
3. **Continuous Improvement:** We continuously monitor our post-market performance, user feedback, and clinical safety events to improve our product, enhance our processes, and maintain the effectiveness of the QMS.

# 3. Framework for Quality Objectives
This Quality Policy provides the framework for defining, measuring, and reviewing our Quality Objectives (documented in QMS-5.4.1-01). Objectives are established by Top Management and reviewed during Management Reviews to ensure they drive continuous improvement and alignment with our strategic direction.

# 4. Communication and Understanding
Top Management ensures this policy is communicated to all personnel through induction training, periodic QMS awareness sessions, and prominent display within the company’s internal digital workspaces. Personnel are required to understand how their roles contribute to achieving the commitments outlined in this policy.

# 6. Scope and Applicability
This Quality Policy applies to all staff, contractors, and operations at «[INSERT: Organisation Name]» involved in the design, development, deployment, maintenance, and support of the Naomi SaMD.

# 7. Terms and Definitions
*   **Quality Policy:** Overall intentions and direction of an organisation related to quality as formally expressed by Top Management.
*   **Clinical Safety:** Freedom from unacceptable clinical risk in the deployment and operation of health software.

# 8. Responsibilities
*   **Top Management (CEO):** Formulates, approves, communicates, and periodically reviews the Quality Policy (Clause 5.3).
*   **All Employees:** Understand, implement, and maintain compliance with the Quality Policy in their daily work.
