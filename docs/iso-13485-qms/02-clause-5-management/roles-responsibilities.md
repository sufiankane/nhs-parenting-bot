# 1. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define and communicate the interrelation of all personnel who manage, perform, and verify work affecting quality, in compliance with ISO 13485:2016 (Clauses 5.5.1 and 5.5.2). This ensures that roles, responsibilities, and authorities are clearly established within «[INSERT: Organisation Name]» for the development and lifecycle management of the Naomi SaMD.

# 2. Organisational Structure
As a small software organisation (<20 employees), «[INSERT: Organisation Name]» operates a streamlined structure. 
- **Top Management** (CEO/Director) holds ultimate accountability.
- The **QA Manager** (acting as Management Representative) and **Clinical Safety Officer** report directly to Top Management.
- The **Lead Developer** oversees the technical engineering team.
- The **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** ensures compliance with data privacy regulations.

# 3. Key Roles and Authorities

## 3.1 CEO / Director (Top Management)
- Holds ultimate responsibility for the QMS and product safety.
- Ensures the availability of resources necessary to maintain QMS effectiveness.
- Establishes the Quality Policy and Quality Objectives.
- Chairs the Management Review.

## 3.2 Management Representative (QA Manager)
*Appointed by Top Management in accordance with Clause 5.5.2.*
- Ensures that processes needed for the QMS are documented, implemented, and maintained.
- Reports to Top Management on the effectiveness of the QMS and any need for improvement.
- Promotes awareness of regulatory and customer requirements throughout the organisation.
- Acts as the primary liaison with external parties (e.g., auditors, Notified/Approved Bodies) regarding the QMS.

## 3.3 Clinical Safety Officer (CSO)
*Mandatory role per NHS DCB0129/DCB0160.*
- Must be a suitably qualified and experienced clinician with current professional registration.
- Conducts and oversees clinical risk management activities.
- Approves the Clinical Risk Management Plan, Hazard Log, and Clinical Safety Case Report.
- Reviews and signs off on product releases to ensure clinical safety and NHS guidance alignment.

## 3.4 Lead Developer
- Responsible for the design, development, and maintenance of the Naomi application (Cloudflare infrastructure, RAG pipeline, LLM integration).
- Ensures software lifecycle processes (IEC 62304) and cybersecurity controls are implemented.
- Approves technical design documentation and code changes.

## 3.5 Data Protection Officer (DPO)
- Oversees data privacy compliance (UK GDPR) for health data processed by the application.
- Advises on Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) and handles privacy-related incidents.

# 7. Terms and Definitions
*   **Responsibility:** The obligation of an individual to successfully carry out assigned duties.
*   **Authority:** The formal right and power to make decisions, allocate resources, or grant approvals.
*   **RACI:** Responsibility assignment matrix defining Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed roles.
